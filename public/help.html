math-type-x

An equation editor: build a formula with the toolbar or the keyboard, or draw it by hand, then copy it as MathML, LaTeX, SVG or PNG.

Toolbar

The tabs group the symbols and structures. Alt+1 … Alt+9 open the first nine, Alt+0 opens the contextual tab, whose controls depend on where the caret is or what is selected (a fraction, a matrix, a long division, a fence …).

Buttons with a small arrow open a list of related symbols. A button applies to the current selection when there is one, otherwise it inserts at the caret.

Handwriting

The hand button on the right edge of the editor switches to handwriting mode. Write the formula, wait a moment, and it is recognised and placed in the editor. Scribble over a part to delete it.

Languages and writing direction

The editor interface is available in several languages, including Arabic. With Right to left editing switched on, formulas are written and mirrored from right to left.

Output

Under the editor the same formula is shown as MathML, LaTeX, SVG, PNG, accessible text and plain text. Use Copy or Download for the format you need.
